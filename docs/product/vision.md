# Product vision — LaPlaza

## The problem

Information about what is going on in a town or neighborhood (matches, concerts, fairs,
theater, cultural activities, local festivals…) is **scattered and ephemeral**. It lives
in Facebook posts, Instagram stories, printed posters, and WhatsApp groups. As a result:

- People **find out late**, or not at all.
- There is no single place to see, ordered by date, "what's on this week here".
- Discovering events requires following many accounts and depending on their algorithms.

## Target user

**Residents of a locality** who want to easily learn about plans in their area:

- Residents of all ages with a smartphone.
- People who come back to town (weekends, summer, festivals) and want to know what to do.
- Secondary: **local organizations** (clubs, town hall, associations, bars, venues) that
  need to spread the word about their events and today rely only on social media.

## Value proposition

> **A single wall with everything happening near you, ordered by date and filtered by
> what you care about — without having to follow anyone.**

Differentiators versus "everything on social media":

- **Zero friction:** browse the wall **without an account**.
- **Chronological and actionable:** ordered from today into the future, not by algorithm.
- **Personalizable:** filter by interests.
- **Reminders:** when marking **"Me interesa"**, push alerts 1 day and 1 hour before.
- **Quality:** only **manually verified organizations** can publish → less noise and spam.

## The name: LaPlaza

The town **square** ("la plaza") is, historically, the meeting point where you find out
what is happening. LaPlaza brings that idea to mobile: a common, local, and trusted place
to discover what's on nearby. A short, warm, Spanish name.

## Why we start in a small pilot town in Spain

- **Manageable scope:** a small municipality lets us validate the product end to end with
  few organizations and real events.
- **Sharp problem:** in small localities the scattering of information is more painful and
  no dedicated alternative exists.
- **Closeness and trust:** on-the-ground knowledge to verify organizations by hand and to
  seed the initial content (the two-sided cold-start problem).
- **Cheap learning:** validate here before generalizing to other locations.

> The long-term goal is **multi-location**; this pilot location is used to validate the
> model before scaling (see [`roadmap.md`](../roadmap.md)).
